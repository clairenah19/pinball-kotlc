How to play

  • Paddles Hold the left half of the screen for the left paddle, the right half for the right paddle. Use both thumbs. Desktop: arrows or Z / M.
  • Launch The ball launches by itself. No plunger.
  • Light Leap Light all five crystals for a beam, a bonus and up to x5 score.
  • Abilities Hit every ability orb to unlock a multiplier.
  • Foxfire tower Lock three balls for multiball.
  • Black Swan A hidden scoop with a clue or a prize.
  • Neverseen Lights go violet. Hit the flashing shots before they flee with your points.
  • Alicorn wing Spin it enough for a timed bonus.
  • Wizard mode Finish every mode to become Keeper of the Lost Cities.
